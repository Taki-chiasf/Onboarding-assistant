# Organization Structure

## Departments
The company is organized into Engineering, Product, Design, Sales, Marketing,
Finance, People, Legal, and Support.

## Engineering teams
Engineering is split into Platform, Payments, Data, and Infrastructure teams,
each with a tech lead and an engineering manager.

## Reporting
Individual contributors report to a manager within their team. Teams report up
through department leads to the executive team.

## Finding people
Use the assistant or the people directory to look up who is on which team.
