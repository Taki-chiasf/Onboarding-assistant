# Welcome to the Team

Welcome. This handbook answers the questions new hires ask most. Read it in your
first week, and ask your onboarding buddy anything that is unclear.

## Your first day
Complete the new-hire checklist, set up your devices, and join the company-wide
welcome call.

## Your first week
Meet your team, pair with your onboarding buddy, and ship your first small
change. Ask questions freely.

## Where to go for help
Use the assistant for policy and org questions. For anything else, ask in your
team's chat channel.
