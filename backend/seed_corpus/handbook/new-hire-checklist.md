# New Hire Checklist

## Before day one
Complete the HR paperwork, submit your bank details, and review the benefits
overview.

## Week one
Set up your laptop, VPN, SSO, and two-factor authentication. Join your team's
standups. Meet your onboarding buddy.

## Week two
Ship a small change to a production service. Attend the security and compliance
onboarding session.

## Month one
Complete your first performance check-in goals with your manager.
